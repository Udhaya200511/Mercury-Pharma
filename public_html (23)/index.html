About Mercury Pharma

37+ years of trust, dedication and pharmaceutical excellence

Pharmaceutical Excellence Since 1988

Mercury Pharma was founded in 1988 with a vision to ensure affordable and timely healthcare products reach every corner of Tamil Nadu. What started as a small wholesale venture has grown into one of the most trusted pharmaceutical distribution companies in Southern Tamil Nadu.

We are a leading pharmaceutical wholesaler specializing in Generic Medicines, OTC Products, Surgical Products and Healthcare Supplies. We work directly with top pharmaceutical manufacturers and serve hospitals, pharmacies, doctors and stockists across Southern Tamil Nadu.

Our 24×7 customer support and strong network ensures that healthcare professionals never face shortage of critical medicines.
